The problem

Rover risks and the operator’s view

Mission decisions depend on reliable sensing and a clear view of each rover’s condition.

Raised dust on optics

Coated lenses lose contrast and reliable surface points.

Glare & deep shadow

Lighting extremes hide the features used for tracking.

Low-texture terrain

Uniform sand offers few stable visual landmarks.

Soft-sand wheel slip

Wheels turn while actual movement falls behind.

Sensor & link drops

Missing frames or communications interrupt context.
