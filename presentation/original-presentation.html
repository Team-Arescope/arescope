The challenge

Working on Mars, seen from Earth

Our answer: one system from the helmet to Mission Control. A compact sensor module captures terrain, crew and scene, shows it to the astronaut and streams it to Earth.

01
Delayed link

Signals between Mars and Earth take minutes each way.

02
Unknown terrain

No GPS network, only orbital maps of the ground.

03
Limited crew view

Earth sees little of where people are or how they feel.
